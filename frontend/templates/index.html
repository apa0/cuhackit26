What's Happening?

Massive data centers are being built across South Carolina. They consume as much electricity as small cities, but special utility agreements mean their costs are distributed to the whole community.

💸

Who Pays?

You do. SC farmers in counties with data centers pay an average of ${{ avg_with - avg_without }} more per month on electricity than those in unaffected counties.

Why Farmers?

Agricultural operations use significant electricity for irrigation, cold storage, and equipment. A ${{ avg_with - avg_without }}/month overcharge means thousands of dollars less for seed, equipment, and survival each year.

⚖️

What Can We Do?

The SC Public Service Commission sets utility rates. Public pressure, petitions, and town hall attendance have changed outcomes before. Your voice — and your signature — matter.
